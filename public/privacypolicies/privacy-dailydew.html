🌱 DailyDew Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Summary: We care deeply about your privacy. Your plant data stays on your phone. We only use Google Play Services to handle "Plus" upgrades and do not have access to your financial details.

What Data We Collect

DailyDew is designed to be private. Most data is stored locally, but some data is processed for purchases:

  • Purchase History: If you upgrade to DailyDew Plus, we collect a transaction ID and purchase status. This is required to unlock your premium features and allow you to restore them on new devices.
  • Local Data: Your plant list, watering schedules, and settings are stored locally on your device. This data is never sent to our servers.

In-App Purchases & Payments

DailyDew offers a one-time "Plus" unlock. To provide this service:

  • All transactions are processed securely by the Google Play Store.
  • Financial Info: We do not see, collect, or store your credit card numbers, bank information, or billing address.
  • For more information, please see the Google Play Privacy Policy.

What We Don't Do

  • ❌ No third-party tracking or advertising IDs
  • ❌ No selling of user data
  • ❌ No user accounts or login requirements

Deleting Your Data

To delete your local plant data, simply uninstall the app. To manage or inquire about your purchase history, you can contact us or manage your history via the Google Play Store app.
