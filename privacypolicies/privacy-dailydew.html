🌱 DailyDew Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

What Data We Collect

None. This app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information or data to external servers.

How Data Is Stored

All data is stored locally on your device using SharedPreferences. This includes:

  • Your plant list and watering schedules
  • Your name and settings
  • Notification preferences

What We Don't Do

  • ❌ No analytics or tracking
  • ❌ No third-party data sharing
  • ❌ No user accounts or login
  • ❌ No internet connection required (except for optional notifications)

Deleting Your Data

To delete all your data, simply uninstall the app from your device.
