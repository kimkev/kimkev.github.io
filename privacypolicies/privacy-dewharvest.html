🌾 DewHarvest Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Summary: We care deeply about your privacy. Your gameplay data and deck progress stay strictly on your device. We only use Google Play Services to securely process in-app purchases and do not have access to your personal financial details.

What Data We Collect

DewHarvest is built to be private, offline-first, and local. Most data is stored locally, but some basic data is processed when making purchases:

  • Purchase History: If you make an in-app purchase, a transaction ID and purchase status are processed by Google Play to verify, unlock, and restore your purchases across devices.
  • Local Game Data: Your run history, card unlocks, decks, and settings are stored locally on your device. This data is never sent to our servers.

In-App Purchases & Payments

DewHarvest offers optional in-app purchases. To provide this service:

  • All payment transactions are handled directly and securely by the Google Play Store.
  • Financial Info: We do not see, collect, or store your credit card details, bank information, or billing address.
  • For more information on payment processing, please refer to the Google Play Privacy Policy.

Device Permissions

  • Vibration / Haptics: Used strictly to provide tactile feedback during card actions and gameplay.
  • Internet Access: Used solely to verify and restore in-app purchases with Google Play.

What We Don't Do

  • ❌ No third-party ads or tracking IDs
  • ❌ No selling or sharing of user data
  • ❌ No account creation or forced logins

Deleting Your Data

Because all gameplay data is stored strictly on your device, you can delete all local data at any time simply by clearing the app's cache/storage or uninstalling the app. You can manage your purchase history directly inside the Google Play Store app.
